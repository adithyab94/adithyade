Privacy

No cookies. No tracking.

This is a personal blog. I don't collect, store or sell any data about you.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It runs no analytics, ads, tracking pixels or social media embeds.
  • It loads nothing from other servers. Fonts, scripts and images all come from this site.
  • It has no forms, comments or accounts, so it never asks you for personal data.

What your browser keeps

A few features remember small things in your own browser's local storage, only after you use them: unlocking the Physical-AI notes, which stages of a note you've opened, and quiz answers in the notes. This never leaves your device and you can clear it any time in your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web server, Cloudflare has to process your IP address and basic request details to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. I don't have access to those logs and only see anonymous, aggregated visit counts.

Links to other sites

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and others take you to their sites, where their own privacy policies apply.
