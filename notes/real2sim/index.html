Debug order

  1. Pick the method: robot RGB-D scan (explicit geometry) or Gaussian splatting (radiance field).
  2. Find the boundary: capture, pose, depth, fusion or training, export.
  3. Prove inputs: poses in the right convention, depth at metric scale, seed cloud present.
  4. Compare at the nearest artifact: transforms.json, points3d.ply, one depth or normal map. Don't wait for the final render.
  5. Only then tune: ICP gates, loss weights, densification thresholds, SH schedule.
