Two patterns to notice

Dense visual labels (masks, poses, captions, segments) are now mostly model-generated (SAM 3.1, VLMs, FoundationPose) with human audits. Contact modalities (F/T, tactile, audio) are labelled as events with timestamps rather than dense annotations, and they often provide the most reliable success evidence for contact-rich tasks.
