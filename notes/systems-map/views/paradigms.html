Paradigm comparison

VLA vs world action model vs task-specific diffusion policy vs RL policy vs classical pipeline. In real systems these are layers of one stack, not rivals: a classical safety and control layer at the bottom, RL-trained controllers for physics-dominated skills, a VLA or WAM for visuomotor behaviour, and a reasoner on top.

Where world action models sit

Taxonomy after the 2026 WAM surveys: cascaded models predict a future and then extract actions; joint models predict both together. Futures can be pixels (video) or latents (features). Inference cost falls from top-left to bottom-right.

Sources: WAM survey (Sep 2026), awesome-wam-2026 taxonomy. Detail in the frontier entry.
