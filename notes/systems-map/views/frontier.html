Effect on the pipeline structure

The scan did not require new top-level stages. It added sub-areas inside existing stages: world models as data engines and simulators (05, 10), WAMs and steerable/in-context models (07), RL post-training of generalists (08, 13), reward and progress models (04), and runtime monitors (12). The biggest structural change is that world models now appear in five stages and the flywheel feeds RL directly, not only data collection.
